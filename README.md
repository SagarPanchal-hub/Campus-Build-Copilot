# Campus Build Copilot

> An AI-first product concept that challenges student ideas before they waste time building the wrong thing.

Campus Build Copilot helps college students turn a vague idea into a smaller, clearer, more testable MVP.

Instead of simply encouraging an idea, it asks:

- What problem are we actually solving?
- Who is experiencing it?
- What are we assuming?
- What could make this solution fail?
- What is the smallest version worth testing?

## Why I Built This

Students often have ideas for apps, projects, startups, and hackathons, but the first instinct is usually to start building.

That can lead to a common failure:

**building a solution before proving that the problem, user, or proposed approach is actually right.**

Campus Build Copilot is designed to challenge that process.

Its goal is not to predict success or make decisions for the builder.

Its goal is to make the builder's thinking more explicit.

## Core Workflow

```text
Idea
  ↓
Problem
  ↓

  ↓
Assumptions
  ↓
Solution Options
  ↓
Challenge
  ↓
Smallest MVP
  ↓
Human Check


Design principle
The system is designed to challenge assumptions rather than simply encourage the user.
